# Кадры урока 7 «Screen lock. Офис без присмотра»

Кадры сгенерированы моделью Gemini для изображений (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) с листом персонажей `docs/mockups/security/cast.webp` как образцом, по промптам ниже; кадры с текстом, логотипами, лишними героями или пустыми рамками исправлены правкой той же картинки. Чтобы перегенерировать кадр, возьмите общий блок и промпт кадра; после правки картинки проверьте альт-тексты в `text.<язык>.ts` и координаты реплик в `lesson.ts`.

## Общее для каждого промпта

> Flat vector cartoon illustration with dark outlines, warm cream background, pastel palette (mint, peach, mustard, indigo, coral for danger). Calm, friendly office tone for adult professionals, no scare tactics. Characters exactly as on the attached cast sheet: Marina (dark curly hair in a bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, badge on a lanyard), Oleg (red hair, beard, mint sweater, always with a mug), Viktor (grey hair and beard, dark-blue blazer), Ira (purple bob, black hoodie, headset), Stripes the scammer (long nose, thin moustache, black beanie, black-and-white striped top; in any disguise the stripes still show). 4:3 frame. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the picture: no words on screens, doors, signs or papers; screens show only abstract shapes. Leave the top-left or top-right quarter and a strip along the bottom calm, for speech bubbles and a caption added later in HTML.

## Кадры

### `coffee.webp` — шаг `open-door`

> Oleg walks away from his desk towards a coffee machine in the background, mug in hand, cheerful. At his desk the laptop is left open and unlocked (bright screen with abstract shapes). Stripes, disguised as a colleague in a grey office cardigan with his striped top showing at the collar and cuffs, has already sat down in Oleg's chair and is typing, smirking. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Реплика Полосатого — справа сверху, красная рамка.

### `lock.webp` — шаг `lock`

> Marina stands up from her desk with her bag on her shoulder, pressing two keys on the laptop keyboard with one hand. The laptop screen is dark blue and shows only a big simple padlock pictogram in the middle. She looks relaxed. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Реплика Марины — слева сверху.

### `usb.webp` — шаг `usb`

> An office parking lot. Oleg bends down and picks up a small USB stick with a heart-shaped sticker from the asphalt, curious and smiling. Behind a parked car Stripes peeks out with a fishing rod whose line ends right at the USB stick. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Мысль Олега — справа сверху.

### `desk.webp` — шаг `desk` (вопрос)

> Top-down three-quarter view of an empty office desk at lunch time: an open laptop with a bright screen, a yellow sticky note on the monitor edge, a printer next to the desk with a sheet of paper in its output tray, a small USB stick lying near the keyboard, a mug and a plant. The chair is pushed back, nobody is there. Calm, clear, every object easy to see. Caption space along the bottom.

Подпись — внизу.
