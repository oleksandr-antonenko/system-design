# Кадры урока 3 «KeePass. Свой сейф-файл»

Кадры сгенерированы моделью Gemini для изображений (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) с листом персонажей `docs/mockups/security/cast.webp` как образцом, по промптам ниже; кадры с текстом, логотипами, лишними героями или пустыми рамками исправлены правкой той же картинки. Чтобы перегенерировать кадр, возьмите общий блок и промпт кадра; после правки картинки проверьте альт-тексты в `text.<язык>.ts` и координаты реплик в `lesson.ts`.

## Общее для каждого промпта

> Flat vector cartoon illustration with dark outlines, warm cream background, pastel palette (mint, peach, mustard, indigo, coral for danger). Calm, friendly office tone for adult professionals, no scare tactics. Characters exactly as on the attached cast sheet: Marina (dark curly hair in a bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, badge on a lanyard), Oleg (red hair, beard, mint sweater, always with a mug), Viktor (grey hair and beard, dark-blue blazer), Ira (purple bob, black hoodie, headset), Stripes the scammer (long nose, thin moustache, black beanie, black-and-white striped top; in any disguise the stripes still show). 4:3 frame. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the picture: no words on screens, doors, signs or papers; screens show only abstract shapes. Leave the top-left or top-right quarter and a strip along the bottom calm, for speech bubbles and a caption added later in HTML.

## Кадры

### `box.webp` — шаг `own-file`

> Marina sits at her desk with an open laptop. Out of the laptop screen she lifts a small, sturdy metal safe-box the size of a jewellery box, as if the box were a file she is holding: it has a round dial lock and no cloud above it. On the desk there is nothing else but a mug of tea and a plant. Calm and pleased. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Реплика Марины — справа сверху.

### `forgot.webp` — шаг `nobody` (вопрос)

> Oleg sits in front of the same kind of small metal safe-box on his desk, clutching his head: he has forgotten the combination. Ira stands next to him and spreads her hands kindly: she cannot open it either. Above the box a dashed empty cloud outline shows that there is no cloud copy. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Мысль Олега — слева сверху.

### `phrase.webp` — шаг `phrase`

> Marina pins four picture cards in a row on a board like a puzzle: a zebra, a tram, a bottle of kefir, a lighthouse. A thought cloud above her head shows the same four pictures joined into a chain. To the side, a crumpled sticky note in the bin. No letters or numbers on any card. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Справа сверху — карточка сравнения мастер-фраз (`compare`).

### `copies.webp` — шаг `backup`

> Marina holds a small USB stick in one hand; next to her an open office cupboard with a shelf where an identical tiny safe-box already sits. On the desk her laptop shows the same safe-box on its screen as an abstract icon. A thin dashed line connects the box on the laptop with the box in the cupboard. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Реплика Марины — слева сверху.
