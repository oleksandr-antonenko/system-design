# Кадры урока 10 «Access token. Свой пропуск для ИИ»

Кадры сгенерированы моделью Gemini для изображений (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) с листом персонажей `docs/mockups/security/cast.webp` как образцом, по промптам ниже; кадры с текстом, логотипами, лишними героями или пустыми рамками исправлены правкой той же картинки. Чтобы перегенерировать кадр, возьмите общий блок и промпт кадра; после правки картинки проверьте альт-тексты в `text.<язык>.ts` и координаты реплик в `lesson.ts`.

## Общее для каждого промпта

> Flat vector cartoon illustration with dark outlines, warm cream background, pastel palette (mint, peach, mustard, indigo, coral for danger). Calm, friendly office tone for adult professionals, no scare tactics. Characters exactly as on the attached cast sheet: Marina (dark curly hair in a bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, badge on a lanyard), Oleg (red hair, beard, mint sweater, always with a mug), Viktor (grey hair and beard, dark-blue blazer), Ira (purple bob, black hoodie, headset), Stripes the scammer (long nose, thin moustache, black beanie, black-and-white striped top; in any disguise the stripes still show). The AI assistant is a small friendly neutral robot (rounded body, screen face with two dot eyes, no logo and no brand colours of any real AI product). 4:3 frame. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the picture: no words on screens, doors, signs or papers; screens show only abstract shapes. Leave the top-left or top-right quarter and a strip along the bottom calm, for speech bubbles and a caption added later in HTML.

## Кадры

### `dictate.webp` — шаг `not-a-vault`

> Oleg at his laptop holds out a sheet of paper with a key drawn on it (no text) to the friendly assistant robot standing on the desk. The sheet is already being sucked into a tall filing cabinet labelled only with a speech-bubble pictogram — the chat archive — where hundreds of identical sheets are stacked. The robot looks a bit worried. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Реплика Олега — слева сверху.

### `pass.webp` — шаг `own-pass`

> Marina hangs a small guest pass on a lanyard around the robot's neck. The pass shows only pictograms: an eye (read only), one folder, and a tiny calendar with one day circled. The robot salutes happily. Next to Marina, a closed safe with a small key-shaped tag hanging from it (the vault entry). Speech-bubble space top-left (Marina) and top-right (robot).

Реплика Марины — слева сверху, ассистента — справа сверху.

### `three.webp` — шаг `leak` (вопрос)

> Oleg at his laptop; above him float three message clouds with pictograms only: a small chart/table, a closed safe with an arrow, and a document with a gear icon that has little keys spilling out of it. Oleg looks unsure. No text anywhere. Space at the top for the question card to stay readable.

Только плашка рассказчика внизу; варианты — в карточке вопроса.

### `friday.webp` — шаг `friday`

> Friday afternoon (a wall calendar with the last workday highlighted by colour only). The guest pass on the robot's lanyard dissolves into small sparkles; the robot waves goodbye cheerfully. Marina nods, a finished report with a chart on her screen. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Реплика ассистента — справа сверху.
