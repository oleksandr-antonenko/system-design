# Кадры урока 9 «Browser extensions. Что видит расширение»

Кадры сгенерированы моделью Gemini для изображений (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) с листом персонажей `docs/mockups/security/cast.webp` как образцом, по промптам ниже; кадры с текстом, логотипами, лишними героями или пустыми рамками исправлены правкой той же картинки. Чтобы перегенерировать кадр, возьмите общий блок и промпт кадра; после правки картинки проверьте альт-тексты в `text.<язык>.ts` и координаты реплик в `lesson.ts`.

## Общее для каждого промпта

> Flat vector cartoon illustration with dark outlines, warm cream background, pastel palette (mint, peach, mustard, indigo, coral for danger). Calm, friendly office tone for adult professionals, no scare tactics. Characters exactly as on the attached cast sheet: Marina (dark curly hair in a bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, badge on a lanyard), Oleg (red hair, beard, mint sweater, always with a mug), Viktor (grey hair and beard, dark-blue blazer), Ira (purple bob, black hoodie, headset), Stripes the scammer (long nose, thin moustache, black beanie, black-and-white striped top; in any disguise the stripes still show). 4:3 frame. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the picture: no words on screens, doors, signs or papers; screens show only abstract shapes. Leave the top-left or top-right quarter and a strip along the bottom calm, for speech bubbles and a caption added later in HTML.

## Кадры

### `helper.webp` — шаг `helper`

> A huge browser window fills the background like a wall with several tabs along its top edge. A tiny cute helper robot sits on the top bar of the window next to the address bar; it holds a big ring of keys, one key per tab, and every tab has a little keyhole. Marina, at normal size, stands in front of the window looking up at the helper, slightly surprised. No logos, no letters. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Реплика Марины — слева сверху.

### `permissions.webp` — шаг `permissions`

> Marina sits at her laptop; a small install dialog window floats out of the screen, enlarged: a list of three lines with simple icons in front of them (a globe, a single page, a pointer hand) and two buttons at the bottom — abstract shapes only, no words. She reads it with a magnifying glass, thoughtful. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Справа сверху — карточка сравнения разрешений (`compare`).

### `sold.webp` — шаг `sold`

> The same tiny helper robot from the browser stands on a little auction box; a hand gives it to a new owner in exchange for a bag of coins. The new owner is Stripes, and the helper's shiny shell is half open on its back revealing black-and-white stripes underneath. Background: the browser window wall. Speech-bubble space top-right.

Реплика Полосатого — справа сверху, красная рамка.

### `cleanup.webp` — шаг `cleanup` (вопрос)

> Marina stands at a small shelf on the edge of a giant browser window where five tiny helper robots sit in a row, each different (one holds a key, one a little dictionary book, one a coupon scissors, one a paint roller, one a video camera). She gently lifts two of them off the shelf into a recycling box. Calm, tidy, routine. Speech-bubble space top-left.

Реплика Марины — слева сверху.

## Итог

Галерея итога берёт четыре кадра: `helper`, `permissions`, `sold`, `cleanup`.
