# Кадры урока 11 «Incident. Если уже нажали»

Кадры сгенерированы моделью Gemini для изображений (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) с листом персонажей `docs/mockups/security/cast.webp` как образцом, по промптам ниже; кадры с текстом, логотипами, лишними героями или пустыми рамками исправлены правкой той же картинки. Чтобы перегенерировать кадр, возьмите общий блок и промпт кадра; после правки картинки проверьте альт-тексты в `text.<язык>.ts` и координаты реплик в `lesson.ts`.

## Общее для каждого промпта

> Flat vector cartoon illustration with dark outlines, warm cream background, pastel palette (mint, peach, mustard, indigo, coral for danger). Calm, friendly office tone for adult professionals, no scare tactics. Characters exactly as on the attached cast sheet: Marina (dark curly hair in a bun, round glasses, mustard cardigan, badge on a lanyard), Oleg (red hair, beard, mint sweater, always with a mug), Viktor (grey hair and beard, dark-blue blazer), Ira (purple bob, black hoodie, headset), Stripes the scammer (long nose, thin moustache, black beanie, black-and-white striped top; in any disguise the stripes still show). 4:3 frame. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the picture: no words on screens, doors, signs or papers; screens show only abstract shapes. Leave the top-left or top-right quarter and a strip along the bottom calm, for speech bubbles and a caption added later in HTML.

## Кадры

### `blush.webp` — шаг `blush`

> Oleg at his desk, blushing to the ears, palm on his forehead; his mug forgotten. On his monitor the cardboard sign-in door from the phishing lesson (a flat cardboard cut-out shaped like a login screen, propped up with sticks, abstract shapes only). A tiny Stripes silhouette with a butterfly net peeks from behind the cardboard. Speech-bubble space top-left (Oleg's thought).

Мысль Олега — слева сверху.

### `to-ira.webp` — шаг `tell-it`

> Ira's desk with two monitors and her headset on. Oleg walks up, sheepish, holding his mug with both hands. Ira turns her chair towards him with a warm, reassuring smile and an open palm, as if saying "good that you came". Speech-bubble space top-left (Oleg) and top-right (Ira).

Реплика Олега — слева сверху, Иры — справа сверху.

### `door.webp` — шаг `close-door`

> Ira firmly shuts an office door that has a brand-new shiny lock, right in front of Stripes' long nose; Stripes holds up an old golden key that clearly no longer fits, looking baffled. Behind Ira, a laptop screen shows a list of small device icons with one crossed out (abstract, no text). Speech-bubble space top-right (Stripes).

Реплика Полосатого — справа сверху, красная рамка.

### `bin.webp` — шаг `bait-after` (вопрос)

> Oleg holds a shiny bait envelope (with the red exclamation mark and alarm clock from the phishing lesson) above an office waste bin, hesitating. Next to the bin, a small tray marked only with a headset pictogram (IT) and an arrow. No text. Space at the top for the question card.

Только плашка рассказчика внизу; варианты — в карточке вопроса.
