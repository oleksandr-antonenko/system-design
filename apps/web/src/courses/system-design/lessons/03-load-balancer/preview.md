# Preview: Load balancer

<!-- Subject of the preview image. The shared prompt and the style: docs/plans/lesson-previews.md -->

Request pulses from client devices on the left converge into one load balancer: a wide flat hexagonal router module in the centre-left. From it, accent paths fan out evenly to three identical server units arranged in a column on the right. The bottom server is dark with a small muted red status light, and the path to it is faded and broken: it was taken out of rotation by a health check, while the two healthy servers glow and share the load.
